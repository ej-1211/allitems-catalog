全品項 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 8 月 18 日

全品項不需要帳號。你的用餐紀錄、照片與設定都存在你自己的裝置上， 我們沒有伺服器可以接收它們。會離開裝置的只有廣告與購買相關的資訊， 兩者都由第三方（Google、Apple）處理，詳見下方。

一、我們自己蒐集什麼

沒有。這個 App 沒有帳號系統、沒有登入、沒有安裝任何分析或追蹤工具。下列資料只存在你的裝置上，開發者無法讀取：

  • 用餐紀錄：品項、日期、評分、心得、你填的價格
  • 照片：存放在 App 自己的檔案容器內，不會上傳到任何地方
  • 偏好設定與收集進度

這些資料會被 Apple 的 iCloud 裝置備份涵蓋，因此換手機還原得回來。那是 Apple 的備份機制，開發者同樣看不到其中的內容。

二、App 會連到哪裡

1. 菜單更新

App 會向 ej-1211.github.io 下載菜單資料檔（JSON）。這是單向下載，不會上傳任何東西。與所有網路請求一樣，該主機會看到你的 IP 位址。

2. 廣告

免費版會顯示 Google AdMob 的廣告，見第三節。

3. 購買

完整版的購買由 Apple App Store 處理，見第四節。

三、廣告（Google AdMob）

免費版會在部分畫面顯示廣告。為了投放廣告並計算成效，Google 可能蒐集：

  • 廣告識別碼（IDFA／IDFV）
  • 裝置型號、作業系統版本、App 版本等裝置資訊
  • 由 IP 位址推得的粗略地區（不是精確定位；本 App 從不要求定位權限）
  • 廣告的曝光與點擊

本 App 不會跳出「App 追蹤透明度」（ATT）請求，因此在未取得你同意的情況下不會跨 App 追蹤你。Google 如何使用這些資料，請見 Google 的合作夥伴網站與 App 政策。

你可以在 iOS 的「設定 → 隱私權與安全性」中調整追蹤與 Apple 廣告的相關選項。

購買完整版之後，所有廣告都會消失，上述蒐集也隨之停止。

四、購買

完整版是一次買斷，交易全程由 Apple App Store 處理。開發者收不到也看不到你的付款資訊，只會從 Apple 取得「這個 Apple ID 是否擁有這項購買」這一件事，用來決定是否解鎖功能。

五、兒童

本 App 不以 13 歲以下兒童為對象，也不會刻意向兒童蒐集個人資料。

六、你的控制權

  • 刪除：移除 App 就會一併刪除裝置上的所有紀錄與照片
  • 匯出：設定頁可以把紀錄匯出成 JSON 檔（不含照片），也可以匯入回來
  • 移除廣告：購買完整版

七、政策變更

本政策若有變更，會更新本頁最上方的日期。重大變更會在 App 內告知。

八、聯絡方式

對本政策有任何疑問，或想提意見與許願：allitems.app@ej1211.com。App 的設定 → 關於 → 意見與許願會直接開一封寫好收件人的信。

那是一般的電子郵件往來，不是 App 在蒐集資料。信由你自己的郵件程式寄出，我們會看到你寫的內容與你的寄件地址——就跟你寄信給任何人一樣。App 本身沒有把任何東西送到我們的伺服器（我們沒有伺服器）。信件內文預設會附上版本、系統版本與機型三項，方便查問題，你看得到也可以整段刪掉再寄。

AllItems Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

AllItems has no accounts. Your meal records, photos and settings stay on your device — there is no server to receive them. The only information that leaves your device relates to advertising and purchases, both handled by third parties (Google, Apple).

1. What we collect ourselves

Nothing. There is no account system, no sign-in, and no analytics or tracking SDK. The following stays on your device only and is not readable by the developer:

  • Meal records: items, dates, ratings, notes, the prices you enter
  • Photos: stored inside the app's own file container, never uploaded
  • Preferences and collection progress

This data is included in Apple's iCloud device backup, which is how it survives moving to a new phone. That is Apple's mechanism; the developer cannot read its contents.

2. Where the app connects

Menu updates — the app downloads menu data files from ej-1211.github.io. This is a one-way download; nothing is uploaded. As with any network request, that host sees your IP address.

Advertising — see section 3. Purchases — see section 4.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free tier shows ads. To serve and measure them, Google may collect:

  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA/IDFV)
  • Device model, OS version, app version
  • Coarse region inferred from IP address (not precise location; the app never requests location permission)
  • Ad impressions and clicks

The app does not present an App Tracking Transparency prompt, so it does not track you across apps without your consent. See Google's policy on how partner sites and apps use data.

Purchasing the full version removes all ads, and this collection stops with them.

4. Purchases

The full version is a one-time purchase handled entirely by the Apple App Store. The developer never receives or sees your payment details — only whether a given Apple ID owns the purchase, which is used to unlock features.

5. Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Your controls

  • Delete — removing the app deletes all records and photos on the device
  • Export — Settings can export your records as JSON (photos not included) and import them back
  • Remove ads — purchase the full version

7. Changes

Any change updates the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be surfaced in the app.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy, feedback, or feature requests: allitems.app@ej1211.com. Settings → About → Feedback opens a pre-addressed draft in the app.

That is ordinary email, not data collection by the app. The message is sent by your own mail client, so we see what you write and the address you send from — exactly as with any email. The app itself sends nothing to our servers (we do not have any). The draft is pre-filled with your app version, iOS version and device model to help with triage; you can see it and delete it before sending.
